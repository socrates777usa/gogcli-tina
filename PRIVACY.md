# Privacy Policy for gogcli-tina

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

## Who uses this app

`gogcli-tina` is a personal desktop OAuth client used by one person, the owner of the Google account it connects to, for that person's own self-hosted assistant. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app may access the owner's own:

- Gmail messages, labels, filters and settings (read, organize and send)
- Google Calendar events
- Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides files

## How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the account owner asks the assistant to do, such as sending the owner alert emails, filing those alerts under a label, and reading or updating the owner's own files and calendar.

## Storage

OAuth tokens and any data the assistant retrieves are kept only on the owner's own private computers. They are not uploaded to any third-party service by this app.

## Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models outside the owner's own private system.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through this repository's Issues page.
